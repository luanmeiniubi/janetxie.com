About this project

I led this project end-to-end for ScholarHills — from initial concept and storyboarding through visual effects, editing, and sound design. The finished piece is a fully animated infographic commercial, subtitled for accessibility.

Alongside the commercial, I produced a companion video style guide establishing the visual language — motion, color, typography, and pacing — for future ScholarHills video content, so the look and feel can be reproduced consistently by other creators down the line.
