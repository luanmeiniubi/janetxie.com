My work

My work includes eLearning training courses, instructional design, curriculum design, 2.5D infographic animation, video production, and educational technology.

I have expertise in authoring tools such as Articulate Storyline, Rise, Camtasia, Adobe Captivate, Vyond, Final Cut Pro, Adobe Creative Suite (Premiere Pro, Photoshop, After Effects, Illustrator, Lightroom), and Microsoft Office.

My story

I started in film and animation, then realized I could turn storytelling into learning adventures. Now I design courses, games, and tools that make people say "Ohhh, I get it!"

I've built online courses, designed game-based learning, and worked with faculty and global teams to turn complex ideas into engaging, accessible experiences. My approach is simple: keep it fun, inclusive, and learner-centered.

When I'm not designing, I'm probably sketching game ideas, tinkering with LEGO builds, or testing new tools just to see what's possible. Learning should feel less like "training" and more like an experience worth remembering, and that's what I love creating.
