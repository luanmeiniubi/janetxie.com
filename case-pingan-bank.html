Infographic Animation for Ping'An Bank

Topic

About this project

I led the creative direction for this infographic animation, produced for Ping'An Bank to explain how cash deposits work in futures trading — a technical, jargon-heavy topic for most viewers. I owned the full production: storyboarding, visual effects, editing, and sound design, aiming for a piece that's approachable without oversimplifying the mechanics.
